Hobby

My primary project, as of the writing of this paragraph, is modding the game FTL: Faster Than Light. I have plans to learn further game devlopment alongside my academic path.

Nerf Blasters

Ever since I was little, I had quite the eye for Nerf blasters, and enjoyed using them in a variety of situation. Joining the HvZ club at my college campus rekindled that interest, and I have begun to modify and construct them in my offtime to hone my mechanical skills for future classes.

Writing

As my primary creative outlet, being able to bring my ideas to life through text and lore is essential to me being able to function as a person. I have been writing very in-depth documents and backstories, off and on, for years, but most of them remain as unfinished manuscripts I plan to return to.

Music

I listen to a lot of music to keep myself stable, and I have begun to create music as well. I am planning to take drum lessons, and have learned the basics of sound synthesis thanks to a course I recently took.

Academics

UI/UX

The primary focus of my degree course, and a major component of many things I am interested in. An artistic experience, video game, or useful machine is no good if it doesn't feel good to use.

Electronics

Although my skills with electronics are somewhat lacking at the moment, I believe learning to create and use custom electronics would be an exceptional skill to add to my repertoire. It would help me with both career and hobby projects.

Astronomy

Space, and the many absurd things that lie within its infinite depths, have always been a major inspiration to me. Space is a source of wonder. Whenever I look up into the night sky, it always makes me question what there may be out there that we have yet to discover. Needless to say, it's not just fun, but also great writing fuel.
