HUMANS VS. ZOMBIES

The classic college game

Hard AF Image Goes Here

Heart-stopping objectives. Long sprints with dozens of companions. The whirrs of flywheels, the lullaby of a tank, the shouts of each and every player echoing down the halls. A final stand that burns itself into the mind of each and every player, no matter their team. A game of Humans VS Zombies is an experience like no other, showcasing a unique blend of athletics, tactics, sportsmanship, and engineering prowess that reaches the heart of what it means to be a community.

Unleash your inner foam-flinger, or join the horde. The choice is yours.

Founded in 2018, we strive to bring the experience of Humans VS. Zombies to CU Boulder through thick and thin.

We are affiliated with other local Nerf-related organizations, most notably the Rocky Mountain Foam League.
