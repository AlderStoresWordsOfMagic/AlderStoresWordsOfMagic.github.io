Data Policy

×

This website stores data input into its "Past Events" filter. It is saved whenever the date form is updated. When returning to the events page, the saved data is used to keep any previous events you were looking at open; this improves page-to-page consistency and enables quick navigation without losing focus. No other data is stored or used by this site, and all of it is stored locally until the browser cache is cleared. It is inaccessible to us. You can choose to clear it or opt out at any time here.

HUMANS VS. ZOMBIES

The classic college game

Heart-stopping objectives. Long sprints with dozens of companions. The whirrs of flywheels, the lullaby of a tank, the shouts of each and every player echoing down the halls. A final stand that burns itself into the mind of each and every player, no matter their team. A game of Humans VS Zombies is an experience like no other, showcasing a unique blend of athletics, tactics, sportsmanship, and engineering prowess that reaches the heart of what it means to be a community.

Unleash your inner foam-flinger, or join the horde. The choice is yours.

Founded in 2010, we strive to bring the experience of Humans VS. Zombies to CU Boulder through thick and thin. All should be able to enjoy the thrill!

We are affiliated with other local Nerf-related organizations, most notably the Rocky Mountain Foam League.
