Hi, I'm Alder!

I'm a sophomore in CTD, and a web-surfing caterpillar.

About Me

I don't have much of a presence on the Internet right now, and I hope that having a personal website will help me change that in a way I feel comfortable with. My heart lies in my projects, and my mood is quite variable. I prefer identifying without the human element wherever possible, and enjoy the simple pleasure of getting creative.

Thankfully, there's a page that already exists which can help with telling you how I identify. But, long story short, they/them, minimal honorifics, and absolutely zero idea what I'm attracted to. Also high-functioning autism on top of that.

I'm interested in...

Academics

  • UI/UX Design: My degree will be focused on learning how to professionally design websites, user experiences, videogames, heads-up displays, art exhibits, and general applications of design.
  • Electronics: The proper, judicious use of controllers and motors could bring to life a lot of my ideas for physical trinkets, outfits, or other wearable technology.
  • Astronomy: Though secondary, I have always had a vested curiosity about what lies beyond the edge of our atmosphere. Astronomy inspires my creativity.

Hobbies

  • FTL Modding: I got my start in game development through modding the game FTL: Faster Than Light.
  • Nerf Blasters: Ever since I was little, I have always enjoyed watching foam darts and other projectiles fly. The university HvZ club certainly didn't reduce that.
  • Writing: A world resides in my head, one that is in its infancy. Some day, I dream of putting it to paper, and satisfy myself in the meantime with other writing pursuits.
  • Music: Hand-in-hand with writing, I figure it would pair well with how I conceptualize new ideas for my projects. I'm currently seeking opportunities to learn how to drum!

My ends are...

  • Forge a Videogame: If I could tell the stories I wanted to tell, I find it would likely be best done through videogames. There is nothing like planting the player directly in the pilot seat to fully immerse them in the world I have created.
  • Make Your Day: A lot of what motivates me to do what I do is either crushing pressure, or a genuine desire to brighten peoples' days. I want you to leave with words that you will remember, for better or for worse, whether it be from my portfolio or my writing.
  • Become More Comfortable: There are many things that I feel uncomfortable with in regards to my physical form, and how I identify online helps soothe it. I seek to adjust my wardrobe and perhaps create special wearables to feel more comfortable.
