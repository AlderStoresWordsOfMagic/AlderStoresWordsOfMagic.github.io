RULES

Humans VS Zombies can be a rambunctious event full of thrills, chills, and spills. It is important to keep a set of boundaries to avoid injuries and make the experience fun for everyone. Here are the rules you must know.

► Firstly, always remember Rule #1.

DON'T BE A DOUCHEBAG!

  • Don't blast other players in the face.
  • Don't yell at other players for tagging or stunning you.
  • Don't lie about your team affiliation, or whether or not you are stunned.
  • Don't treat players who are out of the game as in the game.
  • Don't show up under the influence of drugs or alcohol.

Rule #1 is paramount to all events, HvZ or no. To not observe it is to bring down yourself and others. Be kind to your fellow players!

General Rules

  1. If you leave the building after an HvZ event has started, you will not be allowed to re-enter.
  2. To participate in an HvZ event, you must receive a bandana that indicates both participation and current team. To receive this bandana, you must attend a rules briefing that covers the content of this page as well as event-specific information.
  3. Moderators wear high-visibility clothing with bright vests, making them easy to identify. They do not participate in the game except where otherwise stated, but enforce rules and run mission objectives. Moderators reserve the right to make on-the-fly rulings for outcomes; arguing with a moderator's call will result in a suspension or ejection from the event.
  4. Do not leave equipment or personal items in any location other than the lobby, and do not take anything that another player owns without their explicit permission.

Playing As A Human

  1. All players start as humans, except for a small number of willing original zombies, or OZs, selected at the start of a round.
  2. Humans always wear their bandanas around their arm.
  3. To fend off zombies, humans may carry verified blasters and throw socks or other small, soft objects.
  4. Humans cannot use melee weapons to stun zombies.

Getting Infected

  1. A human is tagged and becomes a zombie when they are touched by an active zombie. Tagging something a human is holding does not tag the human holding it; you may use your blaster to block a tag.
  2. Once you are tagged, you must return to the lobby, stow your equipment, and tie your bandana around your head. Welcome to the horde.
  3. In the event that you are unsure of a tag, or who struck first in a mutual takedown, have a moderator or witness decide the outcome. If there are no moderators or witnesses, settle the exchange with a quick, decisive competition. Rock-Paper-Scissors is preferred.

Playing As A Zombie

  1. Zombies wear their bandanas around their head.
  2. If a zombie player does not wear their bandana around their head, they are a special infected, or SI, who obeys different rules.
  3. Outside of game-specific or special infected-related exceptions, zombies are not allowed to use shields to block incoming projectiles, or to launch their own to tag humans.

Getting Stunned

  1. A zombie is stunned when hit by a projectile launched by a human. The length of time a zombie is stunned for will be announced per-event. A stunned zombie cannot be stunned again, and becomes active again ("respawns") after their stun timer expires.
  2. Once a zombie is stunned, they may remove their bandana from their head, put their hands on their head, or offer some other visual indicator that they are inactive.
  3. Stunned zombies have the following properties.
    • Stunned zombies cannot tag humans.
    • They cannot interfere with players, and must move out of the way of running players.
    • A stunned zombie can move around while they wait for their stun timer to end.
    • Stunned zombies are still able to collect darts, and are encouraged to do so to bring more back to the lobby.
  4. A stunned zombie must audibly count down the last 10 seconds of their stun timer. While counting, they can be stunned again to restart their stun timer.
  5. A zombie can choose to become active again at any time after their stun timer expires. They do not have to return to activity immediately.

Areas Of Play

  1. Play is allowed in the following areas.
    • First-Level Basement
    • First Floor
    • Second Floor
    • Discovery Learning Center (DLC)
  2. Play is not allowed in the following areas.
    • All classrooms
    • Elevators
  3. Certain zones have special rules.
    • Stairs: Although they are no-play areas, stairwells are fully accessible for movement between floors only. When exiting a stairwell, humans and zombies alike must count out-loud to 2 before resuming gameplay. Spend a maximum of 20 seconds inside a stairwell if possible.
    • Bathrooms: Although they are no-play areas, bathrooms are fully accessible. Use these spaces responsibly. Don't fight in the toilets.
    • The Lobby: The lobby is a safe zone. Players can't be tagged or stunned in the lobby, but they can still exchange equipment, collect refreshments, or potentially interact with the game.
  5. Do not open or close doors that are not tied to a stairwell. Leave them as they are.
  6. Respect other people who are using the Engineering Center at this time. Not everyone here is looking to fling foam, and their studies should remain uninterrupted.

Event Safety

  1. Watch out for your fellow players. Warn them of tripping hazards and potential safety risks.
  2. If a player is hurt, or endangering other players, report it to the nearest moderator immediately.
  3. There will always be a moderator stationed in the lobby at all times. There will also be a Campus Safety Officer in the lobby to keep everyone safe.

Equipment Rules

  1. This event has a blaster FPS cap of 130. All blasters must be verified before use at the chronograph station, located near the lobby.
  2. All blasters must fire foam projectiles only. HPA blasters, blowpipes, slingshots, Zing bows, PVC constructs, and blasters with exposed wiring are not allowed. Realistic-looking blasters are frowned upon and will be buried in embarrassing, brightly colored tape to ensure safety.
  3. Heavyweight darts, vinyl/plastic-tipped darts, and non-foam darts are prohibited. Blasters that fire gel, plastic, water, or metal projectiles are prohibited.
  4. Thrown items cannot have sharp edges or fragment on impact. Very dense items, automated deployables, and items that make strong sounds, visuals, or smells are prohibited.
  5. You must collect all items that are left behind/ejected by your blasters, such as magazines, clips, or shells. These objects tend to be hard plastic, and pose a tripping hazard if loose.
  6. Flashlights are not necessary, and must be checked before use by a moderator. They can still be used if they do not project a greater brightness or volume than a phone flashlight, or have a red filter applied to prevent them from blinding other players. An approved flashlight must not have a strobing feature.

Mission-Specific Rules

This block will be updated per-event to describe rules which apply to the current upcoming game. Some knowledge may be hidden before the event, so these rules are incomplete.

  1. Zombie Purchase: At CU Boulder, loose darts are far more valuable than they seem. Zombies who collect and cleanup spent projectiles can turn them in at a specific booth in the lobby, spending them in exchange for bonuses or even the opportunity to become a special infected!
    • Bonuses are priced by the weight of the darts turned in. The heavier your collection, the more you can buy.
    • Groups of zombies can register a "dart bank" to pool their funds and improve their ability to purchase bonuses.
  2. Respawn Towers: Respawn towers exist in marked areas. Zombies can build these towers, even when stunned, and humans can destroy them.
    • Zombies can use these towers to un-stun themselves much more quickly by standing in their area. If a zombie does not see any humans, they can respawn instantly. If there is a human in their line of sight, however, they must touch the tower and wait 10 seconds to respawn.
  3. Extraction: For each mission, there will be a designated extraction zone.
    • The zone will become active once the humans have completed all objectives necessary to win the game (for missions with objectives) or after the mission's time limit has expired (for missions without objectives).
    • Once at least one human player is within the extraction zone, a moderator will play music on a speaker. The extraction period will last until either no humans are in the zone, or until the music ends.
    • If there are no players in the extraction zone for 5 minutes after an extraction attempt ends, the zombies win. If the music finishes without the humans being pushed out of the extraction zone, the humans win.
  4. Adaptive Difficulty: Special prices and spawn rates will be modified based on human performance between missions. It is likely that a unique special infected will appear if the humans perform well enough.
